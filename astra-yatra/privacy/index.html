Privacy Policy: Astra Yatra

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains how Astra Yatra ("the app", "we") handles information. The app is published by AuraGames, contact: auragames007@gmail.com.

Summary

Astra Yatra does not collect, store on our side, or share any personal data. The game works fully offline. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress (heroes, coins, Punya, settings, Group Board entries and any purchases you own) is stored only on your device. We do not operate servers and do not receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (the Supporter Pack and Switch Tokens) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app receives only a confirmation of what you bought, keeps it on your device so it works offline, and can restore it from Google Play if you reinstall. Payments are covered by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Hero Cards and the Group Board

A Hero Card code contains only your in-game name and game statistics (depth reached, power, rebirths, lineage, top hero and whether you are a Supporter). It is shared only when you choose to share it through your phone's share sheet, and only with the people or apps you pick. Codes you paste from friends are stored only on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships, and the "Last updated" date will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions? Email auragames007@gmail.com.
