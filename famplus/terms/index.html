What FamPlus is

FamPlus lets families build a shared family tree, see how they are related to each other in Telugu and English, and remember birthdays, wedding anniversaries and death anniversaries. FamPlus is free to use, has no ads, and doesn't sell your information. How we handle information is explained in the privacy policy: https://streampoc.github.io/famplus/privacy/.

Who can use it

  • You must be 18 or older to create a family or an account.
  • Parents and relatives can add children to a family tree, but children shouldn't use FamPlus on their own.

Your account

  • You're responsible for what happens on your account. If you lose the device without signing in, we may not be able to recover the account.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings, or by following https://streampoc.github.io/famplus/delete/

What you add

Family trees are about real people, many of whom haven't joined FamPlus themselves. When you add information about anyone, you agree to these rules:

  • Add only what is true to the best of your knowledge, and what your family would be comfortable sharing within the family.
  • Be careful with children's details. FamPlus keeps their date of birth and photo to close family by default.
  • Don't add anything hateful, harassing, sexual, violent or illegal, and don't use FamPlus to embarrass, threaten or track anyone.
  • Don't add photos or text you don't have the right to share.
  • Don't use FamPlus to collect information about people outside your own family, or to spam or advertise.

You keep ownership of what you add. You give AuraGames permission to store it, show it to the members of your family as the app is designed to, and make backups, only so we can run FamPlus for you.

Families, elders and changes

  • Each family is private and invite-only.
  • A family's elders can decide who may change its tree, appoint branch managers, and remove members.
  • Changes are recorded in the family's history and can be undone. Please use this fairly: if you disagree with a change, talk to your family or report it.
  • Anyone can use Report a problem in the app, or email us, about wrong information, a duplicate, information they want removed, or inappropriate content. We may remove content or suspend accounts that break these terms.
