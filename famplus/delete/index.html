Delete your FamPlus account

You can delete your account in the app, or ask us to do it.

In the app

1. Open FamPlus and go to Settings.

2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is removed immediately, together with your date of birth and photo.

Without the app

Email auragames007@gmail.com with the subject Delete my FamPlus account. Tell us your name as it appears in the family tree, and the family's intiperu and village, so we can find you. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted, and what stays

  • Deleted: your account, its sign-in key, your memberships of families, your date of birth and photo.
  • Stays: your place in your family's tree (your name and relationships), because relatives added it and it belongs to the family's record. Ask us in the same email if you want that removed too.
