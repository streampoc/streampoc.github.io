FamPlus privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

FamPlus is a family-tree app made by AuraGames (auragames007@gmail.com). This policy explains what the app stores, why, and how to remove it.

What FamPlus stores

You, and relatives you invite, type in information about your family. FamPlus stores it so the family can build the tree together:

  • About people in the tree:
    • names and surnames (intiperu), including maiden names
    • gender, and who is whose parent, spouse or sibling
    • birth order, birth year or date
    • whether someone has passed away, with the year, date or tithi of passing
    • their family's native village and gotram, if given
    • an optional photo
  • About your account:
    • a random key created on your phone, so the app knows it's you (we store only a scrambled form of it, never the key itself)
    • if you sign in with Google, your Google account ID and your name from Google, so you can open your family on another phone or in a browser (not your email address or anything else from your Google account)
    • which family records you have joined as
    • who in your family made each change, so changes can be undone
    • problem reports you send ("wrong details", "remove me", and so on), so the family's elders and we can act on them

FamPlus does not ask for your phone number or email address, does not read your contacts, and has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Photos come from the system photo picker, so the app only sees the photos you choose.

Reminders for birthdays, wedding anniversaries and death anniversaries (ఆబ్దికం) are scheduled on your phone only.

Who can see it

  • Your family, not the public. A family's tree is visible only to the people who joined it with an invite, and to their spouses. There is no public search.
  • Close family only, if you choose. A person who has joined can limit their date of birth and photo to their close family.
  • Our hosting provider. Cloudflare stores the data for us, as our service provider. We don't sell or share it with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • Encrypted in transit. Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Information about relatives

Family trees are, by nature, about other people too, including children and those who have passed away. Please add only what your family would be comfortable sharing within the family.

If someone in a tree wants their information corrected or removed:

  • they can join the family and edit it themselves; or
  • they can ask a family elder; or
  • they can email us at auragames007@gmail.com, and we will remove it.

Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: Settings › Delete my account. This removes your account immediately, along with your date of birth and photo.
  • Without the app: follow the steps at https://streampoc.github.io/famplus/delete/

People added to a family tree stay in that family's tree after you delete your account, because they belong to the family's record. People removed from a tree are kept in the family's history so a mistake can be undone. Ask us if you want anything erased completely; we do that within 30 days of your request.

Children

FamPlus is meant for adults keeping their family's record. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly let children create accounts.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
