Privacy Policy: Sort & Reveal

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy explains how Sort & Reveal ("the app", "we") handles information. The app is published by AuraGames, contact: auragames007@gmail.com.

Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress (current level, coins, hints, settings and daily streak) is stored only on your device. We do not operate servers and do not receive this data.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information and ad interactions.

This data is processed by Google under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly.
